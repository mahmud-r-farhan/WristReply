# Why On-Device?

WristReply AI runs 100% on your Android phone.

- 🔒 No cloud calls. No analytics.
- 🧠 Google ML Kit Smart Reply model ships with the OS.
- 🪫 15–25 MB resident RAM. 0% idle CPU.
- 🛡️ Manifest declares zero `INTERNET` permission by construction.

See [engineering.md](engineering.md) for the full architecture.
