## 2. Google Play Store Long Description

Tired of typing the same repetitive responses? WristReply brings on-device, lightning-fast smart suggestions to all your incoming messages.

Whether you are using your Android phone or your connected smartwatch, WristReply generates contextual, intelligent reply pills in real time without ever compromising your privacy.

⚡ 100% PRIVATE & OFFLINE
Powered by Google ML Kit, all message processing happens locally on your chipset. No internet connection required, no cloud servers, and zero data tracking.

✨ KEY FEATURES:
• Instant Smart Replies: Get contextual response pills for WhatsApp, Telegram, Signal, and SMS.
• Wearable Sync: Seamlessly receive reply suggestions directly on your Wear OS watch.
• One-Tap Dispatch: Tap a suggested pill from your phone notification or watch wrist UI to reply instantly without unlocking your phone.
• Zero Battery Drain: Highly optimized background architecture designed for minimal RAM and battery usage.
• Full Control: Choose which messaging platforms to monitor and customize your quick reply styles.

🔒 PERMISSIONS & PRIVACY DISCLOSURE:
WristReply uses Android's Notification Access to detect incoming chat messages and generate smart suggestions. Your personal conversations are strictly processed on-device and never stored, collected, or uploaded to any server.
